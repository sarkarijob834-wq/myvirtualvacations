Virtual Vacation Ideas at Home: 12 Fun Things to Try

Want a trip without the packing? These virtual vacation ideas turn an ordinary evening into a small journey, using free tours, live cams and games.

Start with a mood

Decide whether you want calm, curiosity or fun. Calm suits a window view or slow walking tour, curiosity suits monuments and maps, and fun suits a guessing game. Naming the mood first stops you from endlessly browsing and gives the session a shape.

Twelve ideas to try

Pick one and give it twenty minutes.

  • Walk a city at night
  • Watch a sunrise on a live cam
  • Take a flying tour over a coastline
  • Drive a scenic route with music
  • Play a city guessing game
  • Visit one monument and read its story
  • Cook a dish from the city you explore
  • Learn five phrases in its language
  • Compare two cities side by side
  • Keep a travel journal
  • Plan a real trip from your notes
  • Host a virtual trip night with friends

Make it feel real

Use the biggest screen you have, wear headphones and dim the lights. Street sound sells the illusion. Add a snack or drink from the country you are visiting and silence notifications for the session.

Turn it into a weekly habit

One short session each week builds a mental map of the world. Try our 7-day virtual vacation plan for a ready-made rhythm, then repeat your favourite days.

Travel with other people

Invite family or friends to pick a city in turns. Everyone guesses what they will see before the tour starts, then compares. It works in the same room or over a video call.

Keep expectations realistic

A virtual vacation shows sights and sounds, not tastes, smells or people. Treat it as a complement to real travel, not a replacement, and enjoy what it does well.

FAQ

Are these ideas free?

Many tours and live cams are free to start, but check the current terms of any site you use.

How long should a session last?

Twenty to thirty minutes is a good start.

Can I do this on a phone?

Yes, but a larger screen feels more immersive.

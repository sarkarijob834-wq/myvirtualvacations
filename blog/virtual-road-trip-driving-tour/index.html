Virtual Road Trip: Take a Driving Tour from Home

A virtual road trip lets you cruise through cities and between them from a car's viewpoint. Here is how to plan one.

Driving tour basics

A driving tour shows a city from the road. Public descriptions of the Virtual Vacation site list driving tours for 100+ cities and a road trip mode, though features can change.

Pick your route

Choose scenery you love: coastlines, downtown skylines or quiet suburbs. Pairing two cities gives your trip a start and an end.

Add music and snacks

Create a playlist for the region and add road-trip snacks. Sound makes a big difference to the mood.

Use the right screen

A TV or large monitor gives a windscreen feel. Sit back, relax and avoid clicking too much.

Learn as you go

Spot road signs, lane rules and vehicle types. Compare driving styles between countries.

Combine with other tours

Follow a drive with a walking tour of an area you liked or a flying tour for the big picture.

FAQ

Is it the same as a driving game?

No, it uses real footage, not a game engine.

Can I choose the speed?

Controls vary by site.

Is it good for planning a real drive?

Only as a first look; check current conditions.
